cocotb_benches.dau_fixed_point_ln_tb¶

The rendered ln tile against the model it was rendered from.

Zero tolerance: the HDL mirrors LnDatapath.evaluate step for step, so any difference is a defect in one of them. Deviation from correctly rounded ln is a separate, measured question answered in test_fixed_point_ln – this operating point is FAITHFUL, not correctly rounded, and that is a fact about the model, not a licence for the device to differ from it.

The powers of two are here for the same reason they are in the model’s sweep: normalisation is what a ln datapath gets wrong if it gets anything wrong, and it fails at the exponent transitions or nowhere.

ln of zero or a negative has no value. The offending row must not come out with a substituted one, and the tile must recover for the next batch.

status_ready is held low for longer than the pipeline is deep on purpose: when the close-out is taken promptly it flushes the pipeline anyway, so the guard on the push is only load-bearing under status backpressure.
